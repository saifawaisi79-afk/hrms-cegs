## 1. Section A1 — Master Alignment Issues Table

**Reference Pattern Baseline:**
- **Outer Shell:** `AppShell` -> `.app-shell > .main-area > .page-content`, where `.main-area` owns outer responsive padding (`clamp(12px, 2vw, 28px)` top, `clamp(12px, 2.5vw, 40px)` inline, `clamp(28px, 4vw, 48px)` bottom; `12px` at `<=1023px`; `10px` at `<=479px`).
- **Page Header:** `<PageHeader>` (`h1.page-header-title`, `p.page-header-purpose`, `.page-header-actions` right-aligned on desktop and cleanly stacked at mobile).
- **Controls & Buttons:** Consistent `44px` height on filter inputs/selects and standard `.btn` buttons (`min-height: 44px`), compact `.btn-sm` / `.btn-icon` honoring explicit heights, clean right alignment of export/action groups with the title/table right edge.
- **Tables & Numeric Cells:** Wrapped cleanly in horizontally scrollable `.tbl-wrap` / `.datasheet-scroll` (`overflow-x: auto`), `white-space: nowrap` on headers/actions, right-aligned numeric/currency columns (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`), matching header vs body cell padding.
- **Modals & Footers:** Viewport-bounded (`max-height: 90vh`, scrollable body, sticky/right-aligned footer with `[Cancel/Secondary, Primary]` or cleanly wrapped mobile buttons).

### 5.5 Option A vs. Option B Comparison & Recommendation

| Aspect | **Option A: Block Login on Wrong Portal (Recommended)** | **Option B: Allow Login + Redirect + One-Time Notice** |
|---|---|---|
| **Session Security & Intent Integrity** | **Strong.** No JWT cookie (`cegs_token`) or `localStorage` session is created when the user picks the wrong portal; enforced server-side (`403 Forbidden`) so DevTools/API bypass is impossible. | **Weak.** Issues valid session despite mismatch; relies on client toast/banner that can be missed or bypassed by API callers. |
| **User Experience** | Clear inline alert on the login card keeps email populated so the user immediately selects the right portal (or sees why access was denied). | Saves one click on accidental mis-clicks, but leaves users confused why they clicked e.g. `Finance & Invoices` and landed inside `/employees`. |
| **Recommendation** | **Choose Option A** — matches the stated requirement ("A user is onboarded for one specific portal") and ensures strict server-side + client-side portal enforcement. | Only suitable if cross-portal login shortcuts for `super_admin` are explicitly desired alongside a banner. |

**Message Copy & Security Trade-Off (Naming the Assigned Portal vs. Generic Message):**
- **Security vs. Usability Trade-Off:**
  - **Naming the assigned portal** (e.g., *"Your account belongs to the **HR Admin** portal, not Finance."*) is **more helpful for genuine internal employees** who forgot which pill to click, **but leaks role/privilege information** to anyone guessing/credential-stuffing a compromised password (revealing whether an account is `Super Admin` or `Finance`).
  - **Generic message** (*"This account is not registered for the **[Selected]** portal. Please sign in through your assigned portal."*) **prevents role enumeration**, only revealing non-membership in the clicked portal while never disclosing the account's actual privilege tier (e.g. `Super Admin`).
- **Recommendation:** Use **Option A with the generic message** server-side (`403`, `{ error: "This account isn't registered for the <Selected> portal. Please sign in through your assigned portal.", code: "PORTAL_MISMATCH" }`), **only returned AFTER valid password verification** (so unauthenticated attackers cannot probe whether an email exists in a portal without the valid password). If internal usability outweighs enumeration risk in a closed company HRMS, confirm in review whether to include the target portal name or add a client-only `"Switch to [Assigned] portal"` button ONLY if approved.
